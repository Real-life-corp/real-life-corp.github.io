# RLRPG Child Safety Standards

**Effective date:** November 21, 2026
**Last updated:** November 21, 2026

RLRPG is a social app operated by **Sébastien Dubé** (Real Life Corp), based in Québec, Canada. This page describes our standards against **child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)** and how we prevent, detect and respond to it.

We have **zero tolerance** for child sexual abuse and exploitation. These standards apply to every part of the Service: posts, photos, captions, comments, private messages, profiles and quests.

---

## 1. What is prohibited

The following is strictly prohibited on RLRPG:

- **Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)**: any image, video, drawing, AI-generated image or text that depicts or sexualizes a minor, whether real or fictional.
- **Sexualization of minors** in any form, including suggestive photos, comments or captions about minors.
- **Grooming**: contacting a minor to build trust with the aim of sexual contact or exploitation, online or offline.
- **Sextortion**: threatening to share intimate images, or pressuring someone to send them.
- **Soliciting** sexual content from a minor, or **sharing or requesting** CSAM or links to it.
- **Trafficking** or any other form of sexual exploitation of a minor.
- Using the Service to **arrange to meet a minor** for sexual purposes.

These rules are part of our [Terms of Use](/rlrpg/terms-of-use/). Breaking them leads to the **immediate and permanent termination** of the account.

## 2. Minimum age

RLRPG is **not intended for children**. Users must be **at least 16 years old** to create an account (see section 2 of the [Terms of Use](/rlrpg/terms-of-use/)). We close accounts that we find belong to someone under this age.

Users must also have the consent of every identifiable person in their photos, and the consent of a parent or guardian for photos of a minor.

## 3. Prevention and detection

- **Automated review of every photo.** Every photo is checked before it is published. An image-analysis model on our servers detects nudity and sexual content, and a second AI review handles the cases the first one cannot decide. Photos flagged as sexual or inappropriate are **never published**.
- **Automatic suspension.** An account that has **3 photos rejected as inappropriate within 30 days** is automatically suspended.
- **Duplicate detection.** Each photo gets a fingerprint (perceptual hash), so a rejected photo cannot simply be uploaded again.
- **Visibility controls and blocking.** Users choose who can see each post, and can **block** any user to stop them from messaging them, commenting on their posts or sending them quests.

## 4. How to report

Anyone can report content or behavior that puts a child at risk:

- **In the App**: use the **Report** option on the post or the profile concerned. [[TODO: exact path, e.g. "tap ⋯ then Report".]]
- **By email**: write to **reallifecorpo@gmail.com**, with "Child safety" in the subject. Include the username of the account and a description or screenshot of the content. You do not need an RLRPG account to report by email.

If a child is in **immediate danger**, contact your **local police** (911 in Canada and the United States) first.

You can also report child sexual exploitation directly to:

- **Canada**: [Cybertip.ca](https://www.cybertip.ca), the national tipline of the Canadian Centre for Child Protection;
- **United States**: the [NCMEC CyberTipline](https://report.cybertip.org);
- **Elsewhere**: your national hotline, listed by [INHOPE](https://www.inhope.org).

## 5. How we respond

When we receive a report or detect content related to child sexual abuse or exploitation, we:

1. **Review it as a priority**, within **24 hours**.
2. **Remove the content** so that no one can see it.
3. **Terminate the account** responsible, permanently.
4. **Report it to the authorities.** As required by Canadian law, we report child sexual abuse material to **Cybertip.ca** and notify **law enforcement**. When the content or the people involved are connected to the United States, we also report to the **NCMEC CyberTipline**.
5. **Preserve the evidence** for the period required by law, so the authorities can investigate. Preserved content is kept securely and is not accessible to other users.
6. **Cooperate** with law enforcement requests.

## 6. Compliance with the law

We comply with the child safety laws that apply to us, including Canada's *Act respecting the mandatory reporting of Internet child pornography by persons who provide an Internet service* and the *Criminal Code*, as well as the requirements of the Google Play and Apple App Store policies.
