# RLRPG Support

Need help with RLRPG? Write to us at **reallifecorpo@gmail.com** and we will get back to you as soon as possible. Please include your username and a short description of the problem, with a screenshot if you can.

---

## Frequently asked questions

### I did not receive the confirmation email

Check your **spam or junk folder**. The email can take a few minutes to arrive. If you still cannot find it, request a new one from the login page, or contact us.

### I forgot my password

On the login screen, tap **Forgot password?** and enter your email address. You will receive a link to choose a new password.

### How do I block someone?

On the user's profile, tap the **⋯** button, then **Block**. You can unblock people in **Settings** > **Blocked users**.

### How do I report content or a user?

Write to **reallifecorpo@gmail.com** with the username of the account and a description or screenshot of the content. For anything that puts a child at risk, see our [Child Safety Standards](/rlrpg/child-safety/).

### How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to **Settings** > **Delete account**. Your account and your content are deleted as described in our [Privacy Policy](/rlrpg/privacy-policy/). If you can no longer access the app, write to us from the email address of your account.
